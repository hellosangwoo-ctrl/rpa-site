What it does

The client book that keeps itself

You are the front desk, the artist and the bookkeeper. VI One takes the remembering.

🗂️

One card per client

Notes, photos, phone, Instagram handle and the full visit history on a single client profile. Write it once; it stays current.

🏅

Tiers that update themselves

New, Regular, VIP and At risk are set from real visits and real spend — not from a tag you meant to add three months ago.

⏳

A heads-up before they drift

VI One learns each client's own rhythm. When someone who books every four weeks reaches week seven, you hear about it while a message still works.

📉

Spending less, quietly

The client who still comes in but books the cheaper service. VI One watches average ticket per client and flags the slide before it becomes a goodbye.

📅

Appointment book

Your own menu and your own prices. Request → confirmed → done, with no-shows recorded against the client so the pattern is visible.

💵

Sales and revenue

Log a sale in seconds. Per-client totals and this month's revenue, without opening a spreadsheet at midnight.

🎙️

Call notes, written for you

Record a client call and VI One writes the summary and the keywords onto their card — so "she wants to move her fill to Saturday" is still there next month.

Record calls only where you are permitted to and with everyone's knowledge.

📸

Instagram DM in one tap

Most new clients find you there. Open a client's DM thread straight from their card instead of hunting the handle.

Before you download

What VI One does not do in the US

Some of what VI One does in Korea is not switched on here yet. You should know that before you download, not after.

  • ✕It is not a POS. VI One records what you were paid and what for. It does not take the payment or touch your card processor.
  • ✕Automatic text reminders are not live in the US. They run in Korea today. US texting needs a carrier-verified number, which is in progress — until it is done, you send reminders yourself.
  • ✕No AI auto-reply yet. The AI that answers repeat questions needs an inbound number we do not have in the US yet. The AI that summarizes your recorded calls works today.
  • ✕No KakaoTalk. That is a Korean messaging channel. It is simply not on your screen here.
